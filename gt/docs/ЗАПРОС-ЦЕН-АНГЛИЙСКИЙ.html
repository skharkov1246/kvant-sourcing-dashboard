Чего в документе нет и почему. Ни одной цены: это запрос, а не оферта. Ни одной оценки: оценку в запросе печатать нельзя — продавец подстроится под неё. Английские наименования взяты из восстановленного первоисточника заявки и не переписывались: если в оригинале «RESISTANCE TEMPERATURE TRANSMITTER», в запрос идёт именно это, а не обратный перевод русской строки «сопротивление датчика температуры».

Request for quotation

Dear Sirs, please quote the items listed below. The part numbers are the customer's own numbers as they appear in the specification; descriptions are given as printed in the original English parts list.

Please state for every line:

  1. Unit price and total price — for the full quantity stated, not for one piece
  2. Confirmed stock as a number — how many pieces you hold today, not «in stock»
  3. Lead time for the full quantity — counted from order, split by batches if needed
  4. Validity of the quotation — date until which the price holds
  5. Incoterms and place of delivery
  6. Origin of goods and HS code — country of manufacture, not of shipment
  7. Original or equivalent — state clearly if the item offered is not OEM-original, and give its own part number and maker
  8. Net weight and dimensions per item — needed for freight, not for the price
  9. Return conditions — whether the item is returnable and non-cancelable

Two notes that save a round of e-mails. If an item is not available, write «no» against that line rather than omitting it: a missing line is read here as «not answered». If you can offer an equivalent instead of the original, quote both and mark which is which — an equivalent is acceptable only when its own part number and maker are stated.
